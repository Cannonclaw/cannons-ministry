Be Ready Before It Hits
Three things every Mississippi household should do now

Build a Kit

3+ days per person
  • Water — 1 gallon per person, per day
  • Food — 3+ days non-perishable
  • Meds — 7-day supply + prescriptions
  • Power — flashlights, batteries, chargers
  • Docs — IDs, insurance, cash in a dry bag
  • Radio — battery or hand-crank NOAA radio

Have a Plan

Know before the warning
  • Know your zone — look up your evacuation zone today
  • Two routes — plan a primary and a backup out
  • Meet-up — pick a place family reunites
  • Fuel up — keep the tank above half
  • Pets — food, carrier, vet records ready
  • Home — clear gutters, secure loose items

Stay Informed

Trust official sources
  • Warnings — weather.gov & NOAA Weather Radio
  • The storm — hurricanes.gov (Nat'l Hurricane Center)
  • State — MEMA: msema.org · @MSEMA
  • Prepare — ready.gov/hurricanes
  • Emergency — call 911; info line 211
  • When told to go, go. Don't wait out a warning.
